MECHANISM RANKING
  mechanism      worst device   magnitude
  ------------   ------------   ---------
  PMOS NBTI      MP2            4.92e-03
  PMOS HCI       MP1            1.95e-04
  NMOS HCI       -              zero

PMOS NBTI dominates, 25x larger than PMOS hot-carrier degradation.
NMOS hot-carrier degradation is absent.

MP2 leads on NBTI because it is diode-connected, so V_GS = V_DS.
MP1 leads on HCI because it carries the largest V_DS.
The rankings invert: NBTI scales with the vertical gate field, HCI with
the lateral drain field.

WHY THE DEGRADATION IS SMALL
Every PMOS operates at |V_GS| = 366 mV and roughly 95 nA. Neither
mechanism has the field it requires. Subthreshold operation confers
intrinsic reliability - the result is small for a structural reason,
not by chance.

CONTEXT
  Process + mismatch 1 sigma    9.07 mV
  10-year aging drift           9.90 mV
  Corner spread                 55.65 mV
Aging costs approximately one sigma of process spread. It is comparable
to manufacturing variation, not dominant over it.

Drift is larger cold than hot, so aging slightly improves the temperature
coefficient by pulling the cold end down more than the hot end.

LIMITATIONS
Run at nominal supply only, two temperatures. Degradation under supply
stress or duty-cycled operation was not explored.
